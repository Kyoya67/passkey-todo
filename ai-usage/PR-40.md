- 使用箇所：`backend/Dockerfile.migration`、`backend/db/migration-entrypoint.sh`、`infra/modules/ecr/`、`infra/modules/ecs/`、`infra/modules/iam/`、`Makefile`、`README.md`、`DESIGN.md`
